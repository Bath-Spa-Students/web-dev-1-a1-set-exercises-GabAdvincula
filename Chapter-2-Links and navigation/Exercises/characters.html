SPIDERMAN

ABOUT

  • Real Name: Peter Parker, a teen from Queens with spider-like abilities.

  • Powers: Superhuman strength, agility, wall-crawling, and "spider-sense."

  • Alter Ego: Miles Morales is another character who takes on the Spider-Man mantle.

KING PIN

ABOUT

  • Real Name: Wilson Fisk, a powerful crime lord in New York City.

  • Abilities: Incredibly strong, expert combatant, and master strategist.

  • Enemies: Frequent adversary of Spider-Man and Daredevil.

SPIDER-GWEN

ABOUT

  • Real Name: Gwen Stacy from an alternate universe where she gains spider powers.

  • Powers: Similar to Spider-Man with superhuman strength, agility, and "spider-sense."

  • Hobby: Drummer for a band called The Mary Janes.
